Ship it.

Publishing is part of the job. Run the launch checklist, submit your build, and your game goes live in the Student Arcade after review.

Five steps to launch

1

Export a web build

Unity / Godot / GDevelop: an HTML5 or WebGL export. Scratch: share the project.

2

Zip it

Zip the whole build folder. index.html must be at the top level of the zip.

3

Submit the form

Upload the zip, cover image, pitch, controls and credits.

4

Teacher review

Checked against the launch checklist. Fixes come back as feedback.

5

Live in the Arcade

Your game gets its own page, playable right in the browser.

Launch checklist

Every box checked before you submit.

  • Playable start to finish — no soft-locks or crashes
  • Title screen with controls and how to play
  • Credits screen — every team member and every borrowed asset, with its license
  • School-appropriate content
  • No personal info — first name and last initial only
  • Cover image, 630 × 500, made from the cover template
  • Build size under 100 MB
  • Playtested by at least three people outside your team

Submit your build

Have your zipped build, cover image and credits ready. You'll sign in with your school account.

Open the submission form Download the cover template

Unity WebGL settings

Player Settings → Publishing Settings: set Compression Format to Disabled, or keep Gzip and tick Decompression Fallback. Without this, the build won't load on our site.
